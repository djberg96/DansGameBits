\subsection{COMMENTS ON THIS VERSION}

This version uses the S\&T Magazine edition of the rules as its base text. Nearly all of that text has been kept intact, apart from occasional typo corrections and formatting changes.

The Avalon Hill (AH) edition was consulted as a secondary rules source. Material adopted from that edition includes the Rail Cut rules; treatment of 0-10 units; clarifications concerning Overrun supply, Zones of Control, retreats, and advances after combat; the effect of Soviet Interdiction in hex 0120; the order in which out-of-Supply and terrain effects are applied; and the use of German Control Markers. AH material incorporated into this document, together with other errata and clarifications, is marked in blue text.

Other differences in the AH edition---particularly those that conflict with the S\&T rules, are internally inconsistent, or would require an editorial choice---have not been automatically incorporated.

Several incorrect cross-references inherited from the original S\&T rules have also been corrected. These corrections are marked in red text.

For errata, please see:

\url{https://www.spigames.net/db_pages/game_panzergruppeguderian.htm}.

As for presentation, I switched from a three-column layout to a two-column layout, and also used a larger font, since I am not constrained by the magazine format and do not need to worry about page count.

I also used my own custom images for the game equipment section. These were originally created using SVG, converted into hi-res PNG files using Inkscape, then scaled down so they fit into the page properly.

This document was created in LaTeX.
