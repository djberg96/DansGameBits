\section{How to Set Up}

Prepare for play by following the numbered steps below in order:

\begin{steps}
  \item Carefully cut the pieces out of their cardboard stringers with a pair of sharp scissors, sorting them by color and type.
  \item Backfold the map against the creases, and lay it out on a smooth, flat surface.
  \item Decide which player will command each of the opposing armies. The Royalist player sits on the north side of the battlefield and the Allied player sits on the south side of the battlefield.
  \item Set up the pieces so that their front faces appear in the numbered hexes on the map that correspond to the set-up hex numbers printed on the front of each piece. For convenience, the Royalist player should set up the Royalist Army---Prince Rupert's and Newcastle's forces---and the Allied player should set up the Allied Army---Fairfax's force, Manchester's force, and Leven's force (the army of the Scottish Kirk).
  \item Place the Game Turn Marker in the 1 box on the Turn Track.
\end{steps}

You are now ready to fight the battle of Marston Moor.
