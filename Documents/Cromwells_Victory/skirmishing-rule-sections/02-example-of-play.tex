\section{Illustrated Example of Play}

All illustrated hexes are clear terrain. During an Allied March Phase,
\textbf{1/Crfrd} will skirmish with \textbf{Urry}. Cromwell's Heavy Horse is
nearby, but its movement must be committed before the skirmish dice are rolled.

\begin{examplebox}{Before: the Allied player plans the probe}

  \counterimage{h-2-2}{images/rulebook-counters/urry-front.pdf}
  \counterimage{h-4-2}{images/rulebook-counters/tillier-front.pdf}

    at (3.75,-1.05) {Allied front};
  \node[font=\sffamily,fill=white,fill opacity=.88,text opacity=1,inner sep=2pt]
    at (3.75,5.35) {Royalist front};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
The Allied player intends to send 1/Crfrd forward against Urry while moving
Cromwell's Heavy Horse adjacent to Urry. Tillier remains nearby but is not the
declared skirmish target.

The Allied player is making a commitment: Cromwell must move into attack
position before anyone knows whether Urry will be disrupted.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}

  \draw[<->,cvblue,line width=1.2pt]
    ($(h-1-1)+(-.58,.34)$) to[out=155,in=135,looseness=1.12]

\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
1/Crfrd pays its normal entry cost, then reserves 2 MPs. The Skirmish---Return
marker occupies its former hex. Cromwell completes his move adjacent to Urry;
all other Allied movement then ends.

Both sides now roll on the 6+ column:
\begin{itemize}
  \item 1/Crfrd rolls \textbf{1}: \textbf{Dd} against Urry.
  \item Urry's Reaction Roll is \textbf{4}: no effect against 1/Crfrd.
\end{itemize}
The results are recorded but not yet applied.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}

\begin{examplebox}{After: withdraw first, then apply the results}
\begin{minipage}[c]{.58\linewidth}
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=.83,>=Latex]
  \hexmap

  \counterimage{h-1-0}{images/rulebook-counters/crfrd-1-front.pdf}
  \counterimage{h-3-1}{images/rulebook-counters/cromwell-heavy-horse.pdf}
  \counterimage{h-2-2}{images/rulebook-counters/urry-back.pdf}
  \counterimage{h-4-2}{images/rulebook-counters/tillier-front.pdf}
  \draw[->,cvred,line width=1.3pt,shorten <=.5cm,shorten >=.5cm]
    (h-3-1) -- (h-2-2);
  \node[font=\sffamily\bfseries\scriptsize,text=white,fill=cvred,rounded corners=1pt,
        inner xsep=4pt,inner ysep=2pt]
    at ($(h-2-2)+(0,1.02)$) {URRY DISRUPTED};
  \node[font=\sffamily\bfseries\scriptsize,text=cvred,align=center,
        fill=white,draw=cvred!55,rounded corners=1pt,
        inner xsep=4pt,inner ysep=2pt]
    at (h-4-1) {NEXT: COMBAT\\PHASE};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
Before either result takes effect, 1/Crfrd returns to the marked hex. The marker
is removed. The recorded results are then applied simultaneously: 1/Crfrd
remains ordered and Urry is flipped to its disrupted side.

The Allied Combat Phase now begins. Cromwell's Heavy Horse is already adjacent
and must attack normally. It may exploit Urry's disruption because its charge
was positioned before the successful skirmish roll. No additional Allied
movement is permitted.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}
