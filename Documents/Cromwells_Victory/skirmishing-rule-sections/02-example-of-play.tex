\section{Illustrated Example of Play}

All illustrated hexes are clear terrain. At the beginning of an Allied March
Phase, the \textbf{Cromwell} leader designates \textbf{1/Crfrd} to skirmish with
\textbf{Urry}. Cromwell's Heavy Horse is nearby, but its movement must be
committed before the skirmish dice are rolled.

\begin{examplebox}{Before: the Allied player plans the probe}

\centering

  \node[font=\sffamily,fill=white,fill opacity=.88,text opacity=1,inner sep=2pt]
    at (3.75,-1.05) {Allied front};
  \node[font=\sffamily,fill=white,fill opacity=.88,text opacity=1,inner sep=2pt]
    at (3.75,5.35) {Royalist front};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
The Cromwell leader designates 1/Crfrd, an ordered Foot unit of his force that
begins adjacent to him. Urry is exactly two hexes away, with an empty hex between
the two units, so it is an eligible target.

The Allied player intends to place the Skirmish marker in that empty hex.
Tillier is not the declared target.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}

  \counterimage{h-0-0}{images/rulebook-counters/cromwell-leader-front.pdf}

  \counterimage{h-4-2}{images/rulebook-counters/tillier-front.pdf}

  \draw[-{Stealth[length=4pt,width=4pt]},cvred,line width=1.05pt]
    ($(h-3-0)+(.12,.08)$) --
    node[pos=.45,right=3pt,font=\sffamily\scriptsize,text=cvred,
         fill=white,inner sep=1.5pt]{move}
    ($(h-3-1)+(.12,-.82)$);

  \draw[<->,cvblue,line width=1.2pt]
    ($(h-1-1)+(-.58,.34)$) to[out=155,in=135,looseness=1.12]
    node[above,font=\sffamily\bfseries\scriptsize,text=cvblue,
         fill=white,inner sep=1.5pt]{fire / react}
    ($(h-2-2)+(-.3,.6)$);
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
The marker is placed in the intervening hex, while 1/Crfrd remains in its
original hex. Other Allied units could pass through the marked hex, but none may
end its movement there. Cromwell's Heavy Horse moves adjacent to Urry; all other
Allied movement then ends.

Both sides now roll:
\begin{itemize}
  \item 1/Crfrd rolls \textbf{2}: Urry will be disrupted.
  \item Urry's Reaction Roll is \textbf{4}: no effect against 1/Crfrd.
\end{itemize}
The results are recorded but not yet applied.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}

\centering

  \counterimage{h-0-0}{images/rulebook-counters/cromwell-leader-front.pdf}
  \counterimage{h-1-0}{images/rulebook-counters/crfrd-1-front.pdf}

  \draw[->,cvred,line width=1.3pt,shorten <=.5cm,shorten >=.5cm]
    (h-3-1) -- (h-2-2);
  \node[font=\sffamily\bfseries\scriptsize,text=white,fill=cvred,rounded corners=1pt,
        inner xsep=4pt,inner ysep=2pt]
    at ($(h-2-2)+(0,1.02)$) {URRY DISRUPTED};
  \node[font=\sffamily\bfseries\scriptsize,text=cvred,align=center,
        fill=white,draw=cvred!55,rounded corners=1pt,
        inner xsep=4pt,inner ysep=2pt]
    at ($(h-4-1)+(.42,-.3)$) {NEXT: COMBAT\\PHASE};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[c]{.38\linewidth}
\small
The recorded results are applied simultaneously: 1/Crfrd remains ordered and
Urry is flipped to its disrupted side. The Skirmish marker is removed.

The Allied Combat Phase now begins. Cromwell's Heavy Horse is already adjacent
and must attack normally. It may exploit Urry's disruption because its charge
was positioned before the successful skirmish roll. No additional Allied
movement is permitted.
\end{minipage}
\end{examplebox}
