\begin{aidbox}{Rally Check \hfill \normalfont\small Rules 6.0}
\fontsize{8.1}{9.2}\selectfont
\textbf{Modified morale target:}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Xr@{}}
  \toprule
  Printed morale & base \\
  Same-force leader in/adjacent & + command \\
  Foot in Close & +2 \\
  Unit in friendly Train & +1 \\
  Unit in enemy Train & $-2$ \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\smallskip
Roll one die; add \textbf{+1 to the roll} in Obscured or Minimal visibility. A modified roll less than or equal to modified morale rallies the unit. Rolls above 6 count as 6.

\textbf{Ineligible if \refnum{6.2}:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Adjacent to an ordered enemy, unless the disrupted unit is Foot in woods, close, or train and every adjacent ordered enemy is Horse.
  \item Part of a demoralized army, unless stacked with a same-force leader or exempt under 6.2/13.2.
\end{itemize}

Every eligible disrupted unit must check once in its friendly Rally phase.
\end{aidbox}
